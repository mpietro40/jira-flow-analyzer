Analysis Summary

Lead Time Metrics Explained

Average Lead Time

The arithmetic mean of all lead times. Useful for capacity planning but can be skewed by outliers.

Median Lead Time

The middle value when all lead times are sorted. More representative of typical performance than average.

85th Percentile

85% of items are completed within this time. Good for setting realistic delivery expectations.

95th Percentile

95% of items are completed within this time. Helps identify outliers and worst-case scenarios.
